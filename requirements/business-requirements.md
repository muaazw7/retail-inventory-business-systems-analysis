# Business Requirements

## Purpose
The purpose of this document is to define the high-level business requirements for the proposed retail inventory management system.

## Business Requirements

### BR-01: Centralized Inventory Management
The organization requires a centralized system for managing product inventory.

### BR-02: Inventory Visibility
Management and authorized employees require visibility into current inventory levels across all products.

### BR-03: Standardized Reporting
The organization requires standardized reporting for sales, inventory turnover, and product performance.

### BR-04: Low-Stock Identification
Users require a method for identifying products that have fallen below defined reorder levels.

### BR-05: Historical Transaction Data
Management requires historical transaction data to support business analysis, reporting, and forecasting.

### BR-06: Supplier Management
The organization requires centralized supplier information associated with purchased products.

### BR-07: Improved Data Accuracy
The organization requires standardized data-entry and validation processes to reduce inventory and reporting errors.

### BR-08: Reduced Manual Reporting
Management requires automated access to inventory and sales reporting to reduce reliance on manually prepared spreadsheets.

### BR-09: Operational Visibility
Management requires KPI reporting that provides visibility into inventory levels, sales activity, product performance, and potential stock shortages.

### BR-10: Scalable Data Management
The organization requires a structured data solution capable of supporting future increases in products, suppliers, transactions, and reporting requirements.
